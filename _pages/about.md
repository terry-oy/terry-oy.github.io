I am a BIM specialist and data engineer at CBIM, part of China Construction Technology Consulting Co., Ltd. My work focuses on advancing Building Information Modeling (BIM) standards and digital project delivery in the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) industry. My current responsibilities include:

* **buildingSMART initiatives:**
  * Localizing the Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) to align with regional practices and Chinese regulatory requirements.
  * Developing bSDD-China, a dedicated chapter of the buildingSMART Data Dictionary tailored for the Chinese market.
* **BIM standards:** Contributing to the development of national and industry-level BIM data standards to improve interoperability and increase automation in project delivery.
* **Research projects:** Leading applied research initiatives that integrate cutting-edge digital and AI technologies into AEC workflows.


Education
------
I hold a Master's degree from the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://www.technion.ac.il/en/), where I conducted research under the supervision of [Prof. Rafael Sacks](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rafaelsacks/).

I earned my Bachelor's degree (Summa Cum Laude) from the [Department of Civil Engineering](https://cee.technion.ac.il/en/) at Technion.
